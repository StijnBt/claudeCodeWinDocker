Before starting any analysis or recommendation, establish:
- **Industry**: e.g. food & beverage, engineering, pharma, chemical, retail, ...
- **Value chain role**: manufacturer, subcontractor, assembler, wholesaler/distributor,
  importer/exporter, retailer, service provider, 3PL, agent/broker, trader,
  or a hybrid combination

This combination determines the relevant BC functional areas, configuration
choices, and demo scenarios.
